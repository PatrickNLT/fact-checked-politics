Legal frame: `docs/research/legal-france.md` section 1 (broadcaster's neighbouring right L.216-1, embedding v. hosting under *Svensson*/*BestWater*/*VG Bild-Kunst*, section 1.6 terms already fetched) and section 3 (TDM exception L.122-5-3 III, opt-out "notamment par des procédés lisibles par machine" and, per R.122-28, "par le recours à des conditions générales d'utilisation"). Terms change without notice; every Appearance imported into the corpus should record which row and which check date it relied on.

Ticket: GitHub issue #11.

Fetch environment: outbound HTTPS through a proxy with a browser User-Agent. Hosts that refused non-browser clients are tagged **UNVERIFIED** rather than guessed: `francetvinfo.fr` / `franceinfo.fr` (Akamai 403 on `robots.txt`), `cnews.fr` (Cloudflare challenge on every page except `robots.txt`), `europe1.fr` (Cloudflare 403 on every page except `robots.txt` and the homepage), `medef.com` (Cloudflare 403), and the LCI YouTube live of the MEDEF debate (YouTube returned no metadata).

**Reading the table for v1.** Every source's official video can be *embedded* through YouTube or Dailymotion where the uploader left embedding on; only Public Sénat, LCP, CNews and BFM/RMC use Dailymotion as the primary carrier, the others use YouTube for clips and their own player for full programmes. Every broadcaster's own player is licensed for "personal"/"non-professional" sites only, which a public citizen site may or may not be (open question in `legal-france.md`). Every large broadcaster that publishes an AI/TDM position has opted out (TF1/LCI and France Télévisions expressly under L.122-5-3, Radio France by CGU and robots header, BFM by robots and reservation of all reproduction), so the TDM exception cannot be relied on for those; the two parliamentary channels and the MEDEF have published no opt-out, and CNews/Europe 1 could not be read. Replay windows are short and unpublished for TF1+ and france.tv, 7–30 days for RMC+, and effectively permanent for Radio France, Public Sénat, LCP, and any YouTube/Dailymotion upload.

## Open questions

1. **Is a public, non-commercial citizen site a "site personnel (édité à titre non professionnel)" (TF1) or an "utilisation … personnelle effectuée par des particuliers" (France Télévisions)?** The wording targets blogs; a searchable fact-checking site is not commercial but is not "personnel" either. Untested; the practical answer is that the broadcaster disables the export rather than sues (legal-france.md 1.5). Prefer YouTube/Dailymotion embeds, whose platform terms are unambiguous.
2. **Does a generic "toute reproduction est interdite" clause (BFMTV, Public Sénat, LCP, Europe 1) count as an opposition "de manière appropriée" under L.122-5-3 III / R.122-28?** R.122-28 accepts CGU as a vehicle, but the clause must express an opposition to TDM; a blanket reservation of reproduction predates and does not name the exception. TF1, France Télévisions and Radio France removed the doubt by naming L.122-5-3 or "text and data mining"; the others have not. No court has ruled. Treat BFM as opted out anyway (robots + database-extraction clause); treat Public Sénat and LCP as "no express opt-out, ask".
3. **Does YouTube's ToS ban on downloading/scraping bind a *non-user* researcher, and does it constitute the *rightholder's* opposition?** The ToS are Google's; the channel owner (the candidate, the party, the broadcaster) is the rightholder. YouTube's creator setting ("third-party training", off by default) is Google's mechanism, not a machine-readable reservation on the work itself. Whether the TDM exception is available for content lawfully streamed from YouTube, provided no copy is kept beyond the mining, remains open; the "lawful access" condition (L.122-5-3 III) is the pinch point when access breaches platform terms.
4. **Who owns the MEDEF debate recording** and under what licence did MEDEF upload the LCI-broadcast programme to its own channel? Not determinable from public pages. Until known, cite the MEDEF upload as the replay and avoid mirroring.
5. **franceinfo.fr robots.txt, cnews.fr and europe1.fr CGU, medef.com mentions légales, laref.org CGU PDF** could not be read from this environment (bot protection / PDF encoding). Someone with a browser should read them and update the rows; until then those cells are UNVERIFIED.
6. **Exact TF1+ and france.tv replay windows** are not published; the only first-party statements are "fenêtres de droits" (TF1+) and "Limiter la durée d'accès" (FTV CGU art. 3). For import planning, read the expiry printed on each programme page on the day of import and log it.
7. **Dailymotion's 6.9 "Streaming only"** and YouTube's download ban both mean that an ASR pipeline must transcribe from a stream it does not store (or store only for the duration of the mining and then delete, per L.122-5-3), and that the pipeline's access must itself be "licit" — see legal-france.md section 3 limits (1) and (3).
