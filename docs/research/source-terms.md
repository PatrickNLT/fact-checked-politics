# Source terms inventory: embedding, TDM opt-out and replay durability per broadcaster

Resolves issue #11. Companion to `legal-france.md` (sections 1 and 3 give the legal frame: L.216-1 broadcaster right, L.122-5-3 III / L.211-3 8° TDM exception, R.122-28 opt-out by CGU or machine-readable signal). Everything below was read from the sources' own terms, `robots.txt`, `/.well-known/tdmrep.json`, HTTP headers and page metadata on **2026-09-05**, from a Linux host in this session. Press coverage was used only to locate the MEDEF debate. Items a site blocked are marked **UNVERIFIED**.

Method: for each domain, `robots.txt`, `/.well-known/tdmrep.json` (plus the linked `tdm-policy.json`), homepage response headers and `<meta>` tags were fetched with curl; CGU pages were converted to text and grepped for « fouille », « intelligence artificielle », « player », « télécharg », « reproduction »; YouTube embed status was read from the `playableInEmbed` flag of a recent video on each channel. Re-run the same checks before each campaign phase, since several CGU changed in 2025–2026 (BFMTV 25 Sept 2025, TF1 Info 27 Oct 2025, TF1+ 30 July 2026, Dailymotion 7 July 2026, RMC+ 1 Sept 2026).

## Executive summary

- **Every large private or public broadcaster has now opted out of TDM in its CGU**, most of them with explicit reference to L.122-5-3 / L.211-3 8°: TF1+ (I.10), TF1 Info/LCI (art. 9), France Télévisions and franceinfo.fr (art. 13, headed `< TDM-RESERVATION: 1>`), CNews (dedicated "droit d'opposition" page, "y compris à des fins non commerciales"), INA (§ 4). Radio France says it in the header of its `robots.txt` ("No text and data mining (TDM) under Article 4") and bans downloading in its CGU. Europe 1 and BFMTV/RMC do not cite the article but forbid automated or software copying and reserve all reproduction. Only **Public Sénat and LCP** publish no TDM opposition at all.
- **Machine-readable signals are thin.** Only `tf1.fr` and `tf1info.fr` serve a TDMRep file (`tdm-reservation: 1`, policy "obtainConsent"). No site sends a `tdm-reservation` header or meta tag. `robots.txt` files block named AI crawlers (BFMTV, TF1, France TV, Radio France, INA, videos.senat.fr) but none carries a generic TDM directive. Under R.122-28 the CGU clause alone suffices, so the absence of signals does not reopen the exception.
- **Embedding**: the public-service players (France TV, franceinfo, Radio France, TF1 group) are exportable only for "utilisation non commerciale et personnelle effectuée par des particuliers" / "sites personnels (édités à titre non professionnel)". YouTube embedding is enabled on every channel that could be checked (LCI regular videos, Public Sénat, France Inter, RN, LFI, LR, PS, Les Écologistes). **The MEDEF debate replay on LCI's channel has embedding disabled (`playableInEmbed:false`)**: it can only be deep-linked.
- **Durability**: YouTube and Dailymotion copies and Radio France podcast pages are effectively permanent (Questions politiques episodes from December 2021 are still served); catch-up players (france.tv, TF1+, RMC BFM Play) are "durée limitée", 7 days free / up to 30 days for TF1+ Premium per TF1's own marketing; BFMTV's replay index currently lists items back to mid-July 2026.
- **Practical verdict for the site**: link (with timestamp) to the official YouTube/Dailymotion copy or replay page for every source; embed only where the owner has left embedding on and the terms do not confine export to personal blogs (YouTube/Dailymotion players of Public Sénat, LCP, parties, and, with the "particulier" caveat, France TV/Radio France); **do not run the local fetch-and-transcribe pipeline on any of TF1/LCI, France TV/franceinfo, Radio France, CNews, INA content, and treat BFMTV/RMC and Europe 1 as opted out in practice.** Only Public Sénat and LCP content is mineable under L.122-5-3 III without a contractual objection (YouTube's own anti-download terms still apply to the YouTube copy). No source publishes an election-specific or fact-checker reuse policy.

## Per-source detail

### BFMTV and RMC (bfmtv.com, rmc.bfmtv.com)

- Terms: "Conditions d'utilisation générales et spécifiques des sites du groupe RMC BFM et des applications associées", `https://www.bfmtv.com/cgu/`, "Date de mise à jour : 25 septembre 2025"; the same document lists `rmc.bfmtv.com`, `rmcplus.fr`, `elections.bfmtv.com` among the "Sites". A separate "Conditions Particulières d'Utilisation" for RMC+ is dated "01 septembre 2026".
- 4.1 « Tous les droits de reproduction et de représentation sont, en tout état de cause, expressément réservés. » 4.2 « ...accessibles exclusivement pour un usage personnel, privé et non commercial. »
- 8.2 the user undertakes « À ne pas utiliser de logiciels ou de procédés destiné à copier les Informations des Sites ... sans l'autorisation expresse et préalable de l'Editeur »
  - « À ne procéder à de courtes citations, analyses et reproductions destinées à des revues de presse ... que dans les limites et conditions fixées par la loi »
  - « À ne pas extraire ou réutiliser, y compris à des fins privées ... une partie substantielle ou non du contenu des bases de données et archives »
  - « Ne pas mettre en place des systèmes ... de nature à réaliser ... une contrefaçon ... y compris de par le recours à des hyperliens ou des techniques dites de framing ».
  - (English: no copying software, quotations only as the law allows, no database extraction even private, framing only if it does not infringe. There is no L.122-5-3 sentence, so the opt-out rests on the general copy ban, which R.122-28 accepts as a CGU-based opposition; the point is arguable but not worth testing.)
- Signals: `robots.txt` (194 lines) allows `*` except `/mon-compte/`, and disallows `/` for a long list of AI agents (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Google-Extended, CCBot, PerplexityBot, Bytespider, Meta-ExternalAgent, ...); "AI Assistants" (ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User, MistralAI-User) get `Disallow: /` with `Allow: /economie/replay-emissions/*` and a few commercial paths. `/.well-known/tdmrep.json` → 403 (S3 AccessDenied).
  - No `tdm-reservation` header or meta on the homepage.
- Replay: `https://www.bfmtv.com/replay-emissions/` and per-show pages (e.g. `/replay-emissions/bfm-politique/`).
  - Article pages carry JSON-LD `embedUrl: https://serviciel.bfmtv.com/videoplayer/embed/876450610001/<id>` (Brightcove, HLS `.m3u8`), i.e. a technically embeddable player, but the CGU grant no export right.
  - Observed index depth 2026-07-12 → 2026-08-30.
  - BFMTV previously had the Parti Pirate's Twitch re-stream of its 2021 debate struck (legal-france § 1.6).
- Verdict: deep-link to the replay page or YouTube; embed YouTube only; do not mine.

## Open points

- Whether a public, non-commercial citizen site is a "site personnel (édité à titre non professionnel)" (TF1) or "utilisation ... personnelle effectuée par des particuliers" (France TV) is the same open question as in legal-france; the wording fits a personal blog better than a searchable transcript archive.
- The Radio France art. 7 contradiction (share widget contemplated, off-site diffusion forbidden) should be put to Radio France in writing before embedding its player.
- Public Sénat's "no hyperlink without authorisation" clause: send a courtesy request; a refusal would not make linking unlawful under Svensson, but the reply documents good faith.
