# Media acquisition from opted-out Sources by download of the official replay

Every large French broadcaster has opted out of the text-and-data-mining exception (L.122-5-3 III, opposition by terms of use under R.122-28): TF1/LCI, France Télévisions, Radio France, CNews and INA expressly, BFMTV/RMC and Europe 1 in practice; only Public Sénat and LCP publish no opposition (`docs/research/source-terms.md`). The seed corpus, every multi-candidate debate since 27 August 2026, sits mostly on the opted-out Sources, starting with the MEDEF debate on LCI. We decided that the transcription pipeline proceeds anyway, on a recorded risk rather than on the exception: the working copy is made by downloading the audio of the Source's own public platform upload (YouTube or Dailymotion, tools such as yt-dlp, logged out, no account), it is never shown to anyone, it is deleted as soon as speech recognition and diarization have run, and what is published is the speaker's words under the posture of ADR 0002, not the broadcaster's programme. The exposure is contractual, under a platform's download clause or a broadcaster's terms never accepted, not a copyright claim, and the step is invisible to the Source: what it sees is the transcript, whose posture is already decided.

## Considered options

- **Off-air recording** from free-to-air television or radio as a private viewer. Rejected as the standing basis: the private-copy exception is written for private use, so it is a different grey zone, not a clean one, and it only works live, which covers announced debates but not archived interviews. Kept as the second fallback (see consequences).
- **Asking for access** (each broadcaster's legal department, INA research access, the Droit à l'Info monitoring feed). Rejected for the reason ADR 0002 rejected consent letters: a written refusal converts tolerated practice into knowing breach for that Source, and a per-Source refusal breaks the balanced-coverage invariant. Public Sénat and LCP need no request. Partnerships stay in the map's fog, not before the site has proved its value.
- **Transcripts typed by ear** from the embedded replay, with no automated copy. Legally the cleanest, since no copy of the programme is ever made, but 25 to 30 hours of speech is not a mechanism for a solo v1. Kept as the last fallback and as the answer to a Source's objection.
- **Restricting v1 to mineable Sources** plus deep links for the rest. Rejected: a reference with no transcript of the biggest debates is hollow and contradicts the seed-corpus definition.
- **Stream capture instead of download** (recording system audio while the player plays). Rejected as the default on cost: real time per Appearance for a difference that is a platform's download clause. Kept as the first fallback where no downloadable copy exists.

## Consequences

- **Fetch ladder.** In order: the Source's own YouTube or Dailymotion upload, audio only, logged out; a download the Source offers itself (Radio France podcast MP3); the Source's replay page when it needs no account (bfmtv.com, publicsenat.fr, lcp.fr). Fallbacks when none applies: stream capture without an account; off-air capture from free-to-air TNT or FM at broadcast time when the debate is announced in advance; otherwise a deep link only and a transcript typed by ear.
- **No accounts.** The pipeline never creates an account or accepts a Source's terms to reach a replay (TF1+, RMC+): accepting terms that carry the express opposition would turn the copy into a knowing breach of a contract the publisher signed.
- **Strict deletion.** The media is deleted the moment speech recognition and diarization finish; only text, timestamps, speaker labels and the provenance record (fetch date, method, media hash, deletion date) survive. Correctors verify against the official replay embedded on the page, never against a kept clip. A re-run with a better model re-fetches from the Source's durable copy, and does not happen if the replay is gone.
- **Acquisition basis on every Appearance** (glossary): `mining-exception` where the Source publishes no opposition (Public Sénat, LCP, parties' own channels), `tolerated` where it does and the copy was made on this posture, `by-ear` where no automated copy was made, `licensed` if a Source ever grants one.
- **Simulcasts and organiser uploads.** The Source stays the producer of the recording (LCI for the MEDEF debate). The copy downloaded and embedded is the most durable official one that allows it, in order: the producer's own platform upload, a co-broadcaster's, the organiser's; the other carriers are recorded on the Appearance.
- **A notice from a Source asking to stop transcribing** is honoured for future imports of that Source, whose Acquisition basis becomes `by-ear`; already-published Transcripts stay unless the notice targets them, in which case the takedown channel applies; the Appearance stays listed with its deep link so coverage stays visibly balanced; the notice goes in the public notices log.
- **Disclosure.** The method page states the pipeline plainly, including that some Sources oppose mining and the site does not rely on the exception for them; the data files record each Appearance's Acquisition basis anyway.
- The transcription prototype exercises the actual first import, the MEDEF debate from LCI's YouTube copy, rather than an easier Public Sénat case.
